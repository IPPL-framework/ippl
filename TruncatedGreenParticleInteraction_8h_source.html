2// Class TruncatedGreenParticleInteraction
3// This class implements the short-range part of the Ewald Green function split by evaluating the
4// gradient of forceConstant * erfc(alpha * r) / r. Below regularization_cutoff, the singular
5// Coulomb contribution is replaced by a linear force that matches continuously at the cutoff.
6// The long-range part is handled by the periodic or open-boundary P3M mesh solver.
7//
8// The pairs come from the ParticleSpatialOverlapLayout of the container. In the moving-source
9// mode every source also has a momentum, and the class adds the lab-frame E and B of each source
10// moving with its own velocity.
11//

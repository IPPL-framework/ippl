Detailed Description

Catalyst PNG extractor for 3D vector fields (e.g., ippl::Field<Vector<T,3>,3>).

Uses glyph-based rendering with adaptive camera and magnitude-driven color/opacity. Driven by pipeline_default.py and compatible with Catalyst Live.

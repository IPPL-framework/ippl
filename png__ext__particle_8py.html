Detailed Description

Catalyst PNG extractor for 3D particles (ParticleContainer/ParticleBase).

Visualizes particle point data with an adaptive camera. Particles are colored by their first custom point attribute (using magnitude for vectors), or solid red when no custom attribute exists. Generates PNG extracts and supports Catalyst Live.

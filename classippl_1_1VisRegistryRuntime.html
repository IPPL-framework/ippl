Detailed Description

Non-templated heterogeneous registry for visualization & steering.

Stores three allowed categories:

  • Scalars (arithmetic types) – for steerable parameters or simple values
  • Particles (deriving from ParticleBaseBase)
  • Fields (ippl::Field<V,Dim,...>)

Provides visitor-based forEach with overload resolution on real concrete type. NOTE: To allow mutation of scalars through steering, store reference_wrapper<T>.

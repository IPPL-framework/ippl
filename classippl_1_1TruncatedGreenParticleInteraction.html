Values of the pair_kernel parameter. OWNED_PARTICLE (default) runs one thread per owned particle and sums in registers (ParticleSpatialOverlapLayout::forEachOwnedParticle); CELL_PAIRS is the per-cell traversal with one atomic per pair (forEachPair). Both visit the same pairs, so the fields agree up to summation order.

Moving-source mode: every source j moves with its own velocity.

The truncated field of source j is evaluated at the separation in its rest frame, \( r'^2 = r^2 + (\mathbf{u}_j \cdot \mathbf{r})^2 \), and transformed to the lab: the electric contribution is \( \gamma_j \) times the static one with \( r' \) in the radial term, and the magnetic one is \( \mathbf{u}_j / (\gamma_j c) \) cross that electric contribution. Pairs are skipped once \( r' \) reaches rcut. With all \( \mathbf{u}_j = 0 \) the field equals the one of the static constructor and B is unchanged. Requires the additional positive parameter 'speed_of_light' c, in the units that make \( \mathbf{B} = \boldsymbol{\beta} \times \mathbf{E} / c \). Three dimensions only.

Parameters
BMagnetic field attribute, where the evaluated field will be added to
PNormalized momentum \( \mathbf{u} = \beta\gamma \) of every particle; ghosts carry it like any other attribute
